STAY Privacy Policy

STAY by Celestify Limited · Effective 4 September 2026

What STAY does

STAY watches you through your iPhone's front camera while you work. If you leave the frame, it says a line you wrote, and keeps getting louder until you come back. That is the whole product, and this policy is short because the app keeps almost nothing.

The camera

  • STAY uses the front camera only, only after you tap Start watching, and only while the app is on screen. The moment STAY goes to the background, the camera stops.
  • Each frame is analysed on your device with Apple's Vision framework to answer one yes/no question: is a person in frame. Nothing else is inferred.
  • No frame is stored, displayed, recorded, or transmitted. There is no video preview, no recording, no upload, and no way for us or anyone else to see what the camera saw.

Data kept on your device

STAY saves your settings locally so they survive relaunch. They never leave the phone:

  • Your warning lines, patience, attitude, voice and never-give-up choices (stay.settings.v1).
  • Whether you have seen the intro screen.
  • A counter of free sessions used, kept in the device Keychain so it survives reinstall.

Session receipts

When you stop watching, STAY shows a card with how long it watched, how many times you left, your longest absence and the worst line it said. These are numbers and text only — never images from the camera. They exist only on screen unless you tap Share, in which case you choose where the card goes.

Purchases

STAY Pro is an auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. STAY checks your subscription status with Apple; it does not run its own accounts, and there is no sign-up.

What STAY does not do

  • No network requests of its own. No analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, no third-party code.
  • No account, email, name, location, contacts, photos or microphone access.
  • Speech is synthesised on the device with Apple's speech engine.

Changes

If STAY ever starts collecting anything, this page will say so first, with a new effective date at the top.
