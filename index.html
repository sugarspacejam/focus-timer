Prop your iPhone facing you, allow camera access, and tap Start watching. Keep STAY open and turn up the volume. In Settings you can choose your lines, attitude, patience and voice.

To restore Pro, open STAY Settings and tap Restore Purchases. To cancel, open your iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. For help, email contact@celestifyltd.com with your iPhone model and iOS version.
